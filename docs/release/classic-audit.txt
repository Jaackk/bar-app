PORNSTAR MARTINI
reference-pornstar-martini
Recommended: 1.5 piece Fresh passionfruit pulp; 60 ml Vanilla vodka; 15 ml Passionfruit liqueur; 15 ml Vanilla syrup; 15 ml Fresh lime juice; 60 ml Champagne (optional side serve)
Technique: Shake · fine strain
Glass: Chilled coupe | Ice: Cubed ice to shake; no serving ice | Garnish: Half a passionfruit
  Scoop pulp from three fruit halves into the shaker.
  Add vodka, liqueur, syrup and lime; shake with ice and fine strain.
  Serve optional Champagne separately; never shake it.
