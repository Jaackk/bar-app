BAR keeps useful bar knowledge and service tools close at hand. Find a cocktail,

REVIEW NOTES DRAFT
No app login or subscription. Core service content works offline. Owner tools:
Home menu > Profile > Unlock owner tools, using the reviewing device's configured
passcode/biometrics. Local owner authentication is not a shared-venue account.
Cancelled authentication leaves maintenance locked. A passcode-enabled device is
required to test owner editing, content imports and restore. Device authentication
uses NSFaceIDUsageDescription; no biometric data is read or stored by BAR.
PhotosPicker grants access only to a selected photo; no broad photo-library or
camera permission is requested. Optional HTTPS image download is user initiated.
No purchases, ads, analytics SDK, account backend or runtime menu monitor.

PRIVACY POLICY DRAFT — PUBLISH AT A WORKING URL BEFORE SUBMISSION
BAR stores your name/preferences, favourites, training history, product photos,
recipes, prep, batches, counts and lists in the app's local storage. It does not
send this operational data to a BAR server. It has no account system, analytics
service or cloud collaboration. Your iPhone's normal backup services may include
app data according to your device settings. Protect exported backups: they include
your photos and operational data and are not separately password-encrypted.

Choosing a photo stores a resized local copy. Loading an external image contacts
the HTTPS host you enter (and its redirects); that host can see your network
address and request details under its own policy. BAR uses an ephemeral session,
limits downloads and stores the resulting image locally. External source links
open websites governed by their own privacy policies. Exports go only to the
destinations you choose in the system Files/share interface.

You can reset local data from authenticated owner tools. A local safety copy is
retained for recovery. Deleting the app removes its local container; copies you
exported or made through device backups remain under your control. BAR never
receives or stores your device passcode or biometric template.
