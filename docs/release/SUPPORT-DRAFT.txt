BAR SUPPORT — DRAFT WEB COPY, NOT PUBLISHED

BAR is a local-first iPhone companion for cocktail knowledge and bar service tools.

Need help?
Contact: hello@raskyjack.com (proposed; verify delivery before publishing).
Include your iPhone model, iOS version, BAR version/build, what you were doing and
what happened. A screenshot can help; remove employee/guest details and private
venue data first. Do not email a full backup unless specifically needed and agreed.

Before an update or device change
Use Profile > backup export to save a copy to Files outside the app. The backup
contains your photos and operational data; keep it somewhere you control. Sharing
a stock/order list is not a complete backup.

Missing or incorrect drink information
Check whether you are viewing Custom or Recommended. Tell us the drink, active
specification and disputed ingredient/method. Venue changes need an approved venue
source. Never rely on an unverified allergen record as an allergen-free claim.

An image is missing
BAR may deliberately show category artwork when a correct product photo is not
available. Owner tools can add a product photo. Check the exact expression and pack.

Lost changes or a save error
Stop making further changes and record the message. Keep existing backup files;
do not reset or delete/reinstall the app as the first troubleshooting step. Contact
support before replacing data with a restore. Do not promise recovery of every loss.

Connectivity
Core recipes and local service tools work offline. Downloading an optional external
image or opening a reference website needs a connection. Lists are not supplier
orders, and BAR does not synchronise live stock between staff devices.

Before publishing: confirm contact delivery, add the responsible developer name,
link the final privacy policy, and test this page from an iPhone without login.
