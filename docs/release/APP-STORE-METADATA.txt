DESCRIPTION
Made for the moments when you need an answer behind the bar.

BAR brings cocktail knowledge and everyday service tools together on your iPhone.
Search drinks and ingredients, check clear measurements and methods, explore wines,
calculate batches and keep preparation, stock-count and restock notes close at hand.
Core service tools work offline.

HOUSE SPECIFICATIONS AND CLASSIC REFERENCES
See your venue's Custom classic specification alongside a researched Recommended
reference. Choose a default or a preference for one drink. Measures, method, glass,
ice and garnish stay easy to find, with source details available when you need them.

PRACTICAL TOOLS FOR SERVICE
Scale a batch and distinguish the premix from additions made in the glass. Save
favourites, revisit recent drinks, practise with flashcards and quizzes, record waste
and share lists with clear quantities and units. Maintain product photos and export
a complete backup to Files.

BAR v1 includes a Hove-focused venue catalogue. It is a local bar companion: it does
not provide shared live inventory, cloud collaboration or automatic supplier orders.
Menu information does not confirm physical stock. Follow your venue's approved
specifications and allergen procedures.
